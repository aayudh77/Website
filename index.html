About

My career hasn't followed a straight line, but it has followed a thread: translation, in the broadest sense. I've turned celebrity gossip into readable copy on deadline, turned a Nepali astrologer's reading into something a client abroad could actually understand and trust, and now I turn strategy into the daily work of a team.

Each role asked the same underlying question — how do you get something true and useful from one person's head into another's, without losing what matters in between? That's the job I keep signing up for, whether the audience is a reader, a client, or my own team.
